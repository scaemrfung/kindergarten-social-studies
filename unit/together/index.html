Unit · L.O. 5 · Citizenship

Working Together

We belong to groups. We share, make rules, and cooperate.

Outcome

Organizing Idea: Citizenship: Understanding local, national, and global issues empowers individual and collective action toward an inclusive society.

Guiding Question: How can people work together in groups?

Learning Outcome: Children relate cooperation and collaboration to achieving common goals.

Lessons

  1. 1We Belong to Groups
  2. 2Here We Are Together: Sharing in Our Class
  3. 3We Have Rules
  4. 4Collaboration Station: Build It Together

Planned evidence

  • Conversation and observation (role-play)
  • Conversation and product (coloured group circles)
  • Observation (cooperation) and conversation; single-point rubric
  • Observation (turn-taking) and conversation

Reassessment: Lesson 18 is the reassessment for cooperation (L.O. 5).
