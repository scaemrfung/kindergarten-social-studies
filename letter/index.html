Dear families,

This year our Kindergarten class has Social Studies once every two weeks, for 30 minutes. We follow Alberta's new Kindergarten Social Studies program. Its theme is culture, tradition, and community.

Here is what we will learn:

  • Fall: belonging to groups, sharing, and class rules. Then places in our community and their symbols.
  • Winter: who we are: our names, the languages we speak, family traditions, and long ago and now.
  • Spring: needs and wants, leaders and helpers, and making a change together.

We read a Big Book in many lessons, such as My Name Is, We Have Rules, and Who Helps?.

How you can help at home:

  • Talk about a family tradition and how often you do it.
  • Share the story of your child's name, if you like.
  • Point out symbols when you are out: a hospital sign, a library, a stop sign.
  • Name a leader or helper you meet together.

Some lessons send home a short sheet, such as a traditions questionnaire. Thank you for helping your child share with our class.

Sincerely,
Your child's Kindergarten teacher
