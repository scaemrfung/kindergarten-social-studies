Places in Our Community · Lesson 5 of 18 · L.O. 1

What Is a Community?

Grade / Subject: Kindergarten Social Studies · Unit: Places in Our Community, lesson 1 of 3 · Time: 30 minutes · Location: classroom

▶ Open Chalkie slides (opens in new tab)Unit printables
30-minute block
05 : 00
Warm-up
Today’s focus. A community is a group of people who live, work, or play together. Communities have many places.
Key words: community, place

Materials

  • A photo of a local street or park (your own or from the L.O. 1 teacher slide deck, slide 3)
  • Places in My Community by Bobbie Kalman (Epic) or a similar book
  • Anchor chart: "Places in Our Community"
  • See, Think, Wonder prompt (PDF)
  • Parent letter: Time and Place (PDF)
  • L.O. 1 conversation and observation checklist (PDF)
5min

Warm-up

Show the community picture for 1 minute with no talking. Then do See, Think, Wonder: "What do you see? What do you think is happening? What do you wonder?"

10min

Teach

▶ Open Chalkie slides (opens in new tab)

  1. Say: "A community is a group of people who live, work, or play together." Children repeat community.
  2. Read Places in My Community (or show slides). Stop at each place: "What do people do here?"
  3. Add each place to the anchor chart with a quick sketch: school, park, fire station, grocery store, library, church.
  4. Ask: "Which of these places have you been to?"
10min

Activity

Quick sketch: each child draws one place in the community they have been to. Scribe the name. Ask: "Who goes there? What do they do?" Send home the parent letter today.

Printables: See, Think, Wonder prompt (PDF); Parent letter: Time and Place (PDF); L.O. 1 conversation and observation checklist (PDF)

5min

Close

Children hold up sketches. Sort them on the floor: places to learn, play, shop, get help. Ask: "What is one place in our community?"

Exit question: “I can name places in my community.” Ask 3 or 4 children to show or tell.

Full lesson plan (teacher and sub)

Same headings and order as the Alberta lesson plan standard. A substitute can teach from this page alone.

Curriculum outcomes

Social Studies K–6 (2025), Kindergarten

Organizing Idea: Time and Place: Exploring the dynamic relationships between people, place, and time supports understanding of perspectives and events to make meaning of the world.

Guiding Question: What are places within a community?

Learning Outcome (L.O. 1): Children examine places in communities.

KUSP focus for this lesson: Knowledge: A variety of places can be part of a community, for example, playgrounds, natural areas, schools, fire stations, police stations, town or city halls. Understanding: Places in communities have distinct symbols. Skills & Procedures: Recognize familiar places in the local community.

Learning intentions

  • We are learning to examine places that make up a community.
  • I can name places in my community.
  • I can tell what people do there.

Success criteria / evidence of learning

  • Names a familiar place in the community and what happens there.
  • Evidence type: Conversation and product (sketch).

Materials and prep

  • A photo of a local street or park (your own or from the L.O. 1 teacher slide deck, slide 3)
  • Places in My Community by Bobbie Kalman (Epic) or a similar book
  • Anchor chart: "Places in Our Community"
  • See, Think, Wonder prompt (PDF)
  • Parent letter: Time and Place (PDF)
  • L.O. 1 conversation and observation checklist (PDF)
  • Prep (10–15 min before class): print or open the files above, set up the room, and open this page on the classroom screen.

Room or space setup

Carpet facing the screen or easel.

Safety notes

  • Movement is walking pace only, in the cleared space, away from tables and walls.
  • Stop signal: say “Freeze, hands on head.” Children stop, look at you, and listen.
  • Scissors and small items: model safe use; collect before the close.
  • Check the class medical alert list in the sub binder.

Differentiation and inclusive supports

  • Support: Picture cards to point to.
  • Extension: Ask: "Which place is important to your family? Why?"
  • ISP/accommodations: Follow ISP supports listed in the class binder.
  • Language learners: show pictures, model first, and post the key words (community, place).

Classroom management cues

  • Attention signal: “Hands on top!” Children answer “That means stop!”
  • Transitions: count down from 5 to move from carpet to tables.
  • Groups: use the posted groups or carpet rows. Hand out materials by table helpers.
  • A child who needs a break may use the calm corner, then rejoin.

Assessment

Tool: Conversation and product (sketch). Use the checklist from the printables. EIPS K–2 levels:

Exit and clean-up

  • Table helpers collect materials. Put papers in the class folder.
  • Return to the carpet or line up for the next class.
