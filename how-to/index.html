How to teach a lesson

  1. Open the year map. The coral card shows this lesson for today's two-week span.
  2. Open the lesson on the classroom screen. Print the linked printables before class (10–15 minutes of prep).
  3. Press Start on the 30-minute block. It counts down warm-up (5), teach (10), activity (10), and close (5). Tap a part to jump to it.
  4. In the Teach step, read the Big Book with the viewer. Use Full screen on the SMART Board. Arrow keys turn pages.
  5. Assess with the observation checklist. Use EIPS K–2 levels: Not Yet Meeting, Meeting, Mastering Expectations.
  6. Press Stamp complete when done. Stamps show on the year map and the Progress page (this device only).

Faith connections

Some lessons include an optional faith connection (for example, Psalm 139:14 with I Am Unique). These are not part of the Alberta program. Use them if they fit your class.

Calendar

Dates come from the EIPS 2026–27 Division Calendar. Weeks with no school are skipped. To change the schedule next year, edit school-year.js.
