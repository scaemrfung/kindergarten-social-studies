Kindergarten · Social Studies · 2026–27

Kindergarten Social Studies

Eighteen 30-minute lessons, one every two weeks, from September to June. Each lesson runs the same way: warm-up, teach (often a Big Book read-aloud), activity, and close.

Class meets once every two weeks. Each lesson is labelled with its two-week span on the EIPS 2026–27 calendar. After Lesson 18 (early June), the last two spans are for review and reassessment.

Year map

The plan: start the year with belonging and rules (L.O. 5), then places in our community (L.O. 1), then self, names, languages, and traditions across December to February (L.O. 2), then needs and wants (L.O. 3), leaders (L.O. 4), and finish with cooperation and a reassessment (L.O. 5). All 11 Big Books are read in the Teach step.
